Drop your own sponsor logos here to replace the built-in ones.

Name the file after the sponsor's id in src/data/sponsors.ts, for example:
  guinness.svg   heineken.png   allianz.svg   tntsports.png

SVG or PNG (transparent background, the logo filling the image). Your file wins over the
simple-icons mark and the generated wordmark everywhere: boards, pitch, kits, screens and menus.
